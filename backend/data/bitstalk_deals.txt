=== GENERAL INFORMATION: Trade-In, Upgrades and Payment ===
Trade-in: BitsTalk accepts any working smartphone for trade-in at any BitsTalk store. Trade-in value is credited to your account or reduces your upfront payment. Typical values: iPhone 14 up to R4,500, iPhone 15 up to R7,000, Galaxy S23 up to R4,000, Galaxy S24 up to R6,000.
Upgrades: Contract customers can upgrade from month 20 (some deals from month 24, see each deal). Check eligibility by dialling *123*3# or in the MyBitsTalk App.
Out-of-bundle rates: Data R0.99 per MB beyond bundle (safety cap can be set in MyBitsTalk App). Calls R1.50 per minute.
Credit vetting: Required for all contracts. Bring ID, 3 months bank statements, proof of address (not older than 3 months).
Device insurance: BitsTalk Device Insurance from R69 per month (theft, loss, accidental damage).
Where to buy: BitsTalk online shop (bitstalk.co.za/shop) with free delivery, any BitsTalk store, or call 135.
Port your number: Keep your existing number when switching to BitsTalk. Porting takes 1-2 business days. Porting bonus: 10GB free data on any contract deal.
